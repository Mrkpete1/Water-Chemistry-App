V5.4.8 - MAINTENANCE AND CLEANING SCHEDULE

Home > Maintenance Checklist & Filter Pressure.
View Schedule & Cleaning Instructions opens the overall reference schedule.

AFTER EACH USE
Clean door gasket, if equipped.

DAILY
Test bromine and pH and record results.
Check and maintain water at the marked operating level; top off as needed.
Clean trainer, if equipped. Daily recommended; weekly is the minimum.

WEEKLY
Replenish bromine tablets on average; check sooner with heavy use.
Test and record total alkalinity and calcium hardness.
Weekly/as-needed shock using existing app workflow.
Clean stainless steel and wipe down accessible pool surfaces.
Trainer cleaning must not be skipped beyond the weekly minimum.

MONTHLY
Clean the filter on average, sooner as flow or installed pressure threshold requires.
Thoroughly clean pool surfaces using compatible products.
350 profile retains monthly main GFCI testing and four-month drain/clean reminder.
Monthly surface cleaning is not a blanket requirement to drain every model.

RECORDS AND EQUIPMENT
Uncheck Door gasket or Trainer if the pool lacks that equipment.
These settings are pool-specific, persist locally, and are included in maintenance
backups. Existing maintenance completions retain their IDs and history.
After-use tasks remain due after each use; one completion does not clear the task
for the rest of the day. Trainer reminders use daily cadence and flag when the
weekly minimum has been exceeded. Monthly reminders use calendar months.
