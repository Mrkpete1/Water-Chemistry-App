Targets

Calcium: minimum 200 ppm
TA: 80–120 ppm
pH: 7.2–7.8
Bromine: 3–5 ppm, aim 4

pH

Do not dose from pH alone. Low pH uses Taylor R-0006 base demand; high pH uses R-0005 acid demand. Use 1–5 drops; larger demand is corrected in stages and retested.

Safety

Make one adjustment at a time. RUN JETS 15 MINUTES → WAIT 30 MINUTES → RETEST before another adjustment. Never mix chemicals. Follow the exact product label. Weight is preferred; volume is approximate.
