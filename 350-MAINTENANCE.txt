V5.4.4 - HYDROWORX 350 MAINTENANCE

Source: HydroWorx 350 User Guide and Owner Manual Rev B, September 2, 2021.
Summary uses PDF pages 30, 31, 34, 35, 36 and 38-40.
The original PDF is not included in this public app package.

CREATE OR EDIT A CUSTOMER LINK
Select the facility pools, expand Pool Manuals & Tips for its 350, and select
HYDROWORX 350 - OWNER MANUAL REV B (2021) in the maintenance profile field.
New 350 selections default to this profile. It can also be assigned to a custom
pool name when that pool is a 350. Do not assign it to another model.
Save and send the updated facility link. Existing history stays on the device.
For older links lacking profiles, an exact 350 model name selects this schedule.
Customers select their pool and open 350 Maintenance Schedule & Instructions
from Your Pool Resources.

SCHEDULE
Daily: bromine and pH tests.
Weekly: tablet supply, shock, TA and calcium tests, stainless steel cleaning.
Monthly: main GFCI test and filter cartridge cleaning/replacement.
Every four months or sooner as use requires: drain and clean.

MODEL-SPECIFIC PROCEDURES
350 filter service uses isolation valves; no RISE water equalization.
Includes pump strainer basket checks, filter air bleeding, feeder settings,
drain/refill and stainless steel care with section/page references.
Pump basket inspection has no fixed interval specified in the manual.
The manual's filter pressure range is identified as configuration-specific.
Manual chemistry order and lithium shock have not replaced the user's current
app chemistry sequence or chlorine/non-chlorine shock choices.

The schedule is a checklist reference, not a due-date tracker or service log.
Manual links and customer tips remain available; sensitive credentials do not
belong in shareable setup links. Restricted manuals should use host access controls.
